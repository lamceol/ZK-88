# Samples

Everything here is public domain (CC0 1.0), so it can be shared freely. Thank you to the people who gave these recordings away.

## Drums

The kick, snare, hi-hat and break samples are made from **Unruly Drums** by
[Karoryfer Samples](https://www.karoryfer.com/), released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain): see `LICENSE-CC0.txt` and
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.unruly-drums

How they were made (`make_samples.py`): the close and room microphones of each hit were mixed
to mono, trimmed and faded; the snares were made drum & bass style: low mud cut, crack and air
boosted, pitched up 4 semitones and shortened (about 80 ms loud, gone by about 160 ms). `break.wav` is an Amen-style break pattern played on the same kit at 170 BPM, then
compressed and lightly saturated like a sampled break. It is **not** the original "Amen,
Brother" recording; only the well-known rhythm is borrowed.

## Sound effects (`make_fx.py`)

- **Air horn** (`horn_short`, `horn_long`): real trumpets from **VSCO 2 Community Edition**
  (https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, CC0) playing an E chord (E4, B4, E5), each tuned from its
  measured pitch, doubled by a second slightly sharp player, driven and EQ'd like a sound-system horn.
  The long blast sags in pitch at the end. The app plays "bap, bap, baaap" on eighth notes.
- **Boom**: VSCO's concert bass drum, timpani and gong, plus Kenney's low-frequency explosion and
  explosion crunch, plus a synthesised sub drop.
- **Laser**: Kenney's large laser with a small bright one on top.
- **Coin**: VSCO glockenspiel tuned to B6 then E7 (in the key of the bass).
- **Power up**: VSCO glockenspiel run upwards, sped up 1.5x.

Kenney sounds: **Sci-Fi Sounds** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds, CC0), taken from the
GitHub copy https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-sci-fi-sounds-for-godot.
